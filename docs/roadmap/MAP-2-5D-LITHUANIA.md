# Karsen 2.5D geography, urban meshes and transport

## Geospatial source and fictional adaptation
The national land outline is sourced from Natural Earth Admin-0 (public domain) through [geo-countries](https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries), using the [countries.geojson](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/geo-countries/master/data/countries.geojson) snapshot. The original Lithuania MultiPolygon and its 562 coordinate vertices are preserved in `assets/maps/lithuania-outline.geojson`. The reproducible script `scripts/build_lithuania.py` projects and simplifies the outline into the 150 × 100 coordinate space used by `MapSystem`, while preserving the original in the checked-in GeoJSON. The Karsen names, city placement within the country, and game-era geography remain fictional. The shaded hills and building heights are **artistic procedural representations**, not measured LiDAR, DEM elevations, photogrammetry, or real Lithuanian 3D building geometry.

## Rendering
`MapSystem.meshForVisual()` builds true 3D vertex arrays `[x,y,z]`, one roof face and exterior wall faces from each existing city footprint. `meshProject()` applies deterministic oblique (2.5D) projection; `settlementExtrusionsMarkup()` batches non-canonical buildings into SVG path groups to keep mobile DOM counts low. Ten real gameplay landmark footprints per settlement remain separate interactive hit targets; rooftop shapes distinguish factories, bureaus, shrines, stations, clinics, schools, and markets. The national scene uses a real clipped Lithuania outline, graphic extruded coastline, stylized relief and 3D city markers. `data-map-depth` toggles between raised and flat representation without affecting game state. Labels remain in screen space, and the original drag, touch zoom and accessible keyboard controls are preserved.

## Integrated transportation mechanics
`routePlan(from,to,preference)` searches existing `KARSEN_ROUTES` with Dijkstra, never inventing a route. Economy minimizes fare, Express prioritizes rail speed at a premium, and Discreet biases away from checkpoints/rail at higher fare. Planned route stops, distances, leg modes, expected fare and checkpoint risk are previewed before the player commits. Fare and authorization are resolved by the existing `scheduleTravel` / `resolveTravel` methods, charged once, without changing locations before arrival. `WorldGameplay` still applies local checkpoint-based fare adjustments and avoids counting the same checkpoint pressure twice in a precomputed route risk. Travel outcomes remain annual, not real-time within-year movement; express lowers fatigue rather than shortening the one-year world-tick minimum.

## Live strategic atlas and migration
The `JOBS`, `UNREST`, and `HEALTH` layers read `WorldSimulation` per-settlement economy, security and public-health state on every map render. Status rings depict actual values; selection displays precise indices. Population figures are taken from `runtime.demographics.population`, not from the immutable original labels. A traveler leaving an old settlement retains their employer contract until arrival, where `EmploymentSystem.resign` ends only contracts tied to another city. This prevents a worker from indefinitely earning wages from an inaccessible workplace. The route booking warns of that employment consequence before the fare is charged. Home/family settlement changes continue through `NpcSystem.syncAfterTravel`.

## Interpretation of risk, distance and 2.5D geometry
The `checkpointProbability` shown on tickets is exactly `permitRisk * 0.45`, which `resolveTravel()` uses for its annual check; it is not misreported as the raw exposure value. `WorldGameplay` is aware of precomputed risk and does not re-apply the same checkpoint pressure. Economy, Express and Discreet weights also reflect per-leg live settlement security. Travel time remains a single annual tick by original design, so Express improves fatigue, not the number of years consumed. Route distances are **existing fictional gameplay units**, not measured Lithuanian road-kilometers. All municipal footprints, building heights, roads, industries, institutional sites, and 2.5D relief are authored/procedural fiction, not Lithuanian real-world cadastral or elevation data. Scale bars explicitly identify schematic geometry to avoid implying false cartographic precision.

## Browser QA
AEROMI Chrome headless (desktop 1450 × 980, mobile 390 × 844) opened the original local game and verified the national Lithuania silhouette, 14 settlement markers, 3 economic/security/health atlas controls, 10 canonical building targets, batched 2.5D roofs, 3 travel fares, actual booking action, and mobile width (390 px document without horizontal overflow). A missing SVG `.lu-green` fill that produced a black block in Krasnava was identified through browser element inspection and corrected. No third-party runtime map server or data dependency is required.
